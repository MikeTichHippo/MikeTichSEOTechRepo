Your homeowner reading guide

Review your coverage when your household changes

When more people share a home, belongings, liability needs, and living arrangements can change. A quick review helps your policy keep up.

Update who lives with you

Some policies define who counts as a household member for coverage purposes. Let your insurer know when someone moves in or out.

Account for added belongings

More people often means more furniture, electronics, and personal items. Check whether your personal property limit still fits.

Think about safety upgrades

Grab bars, better lighting, and childproofing can make a home safer for everyone. Keep records of improvements that change the home’s features.

Questions about coverage when your household changes

Are relatives who move in covered by my policy?

It depends on how your policy defines household members. Review the definitions section and ask about anyone joining the home.

Do I need more liability coverage?

More people and visitors can change your exposure to liability claims. Review your liability limit and ask whether an umbrella policy makes sense.

What if a family member runs a business from home?

Home businesses may need separate coverage for business equipment or liability. Ask before assuming your homeowners policy applies.
