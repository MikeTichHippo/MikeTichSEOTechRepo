Your homeowner reading guide

Keep water where it belongs

Many water problems start small. Regular checks around appliances, plumbing, and drainage can help you spot them early.

Inspect hoses and fixtures

Check washing machine, dishwasher, and refrigerator supply lines for cracks or bulges, and look under sinks for drips or staining. Replace worn hoses before they fail.

Know your main shut-off

Find the main water shut-off valve and make sure everyone in the home can reach and turn it. Acting quickly can limit damage from a burst pipe.

Manage drainage outside

Keep gutters and downspouts clear and make sure water flows away from the foundation. Standing water near the home is worth investigating.

Questions about preventing water damage

Is water damage covered?

Coverage depends on the source. Sudden and accidental damage, such as a burst pipe, is often treated differently from gradual leaks or flooding. Your policy documents explain what applies.

Is flooding covered by homeowners insurance?

Flood is typically excluded from standard homeowners policies and is usually covered through a separate flood policy. Ask about flood options if your area is at risk.

Do leak detectors help?

Water sensors near water heaters, washers, and sinks can alert you to a leak early, which may help limit damage. They do not replace regular checks.
